# Slide design

Start from the user's brand or the supplied theme. Otherwise choose a restrained palette with one dominant background/text pair and one accent. Use theme fonts that are likely to exist in the target environment and leave enough space for substitution.

Each slide should make one main point. Use a clear hierarchy: concise title, limited supporting text, and a visual or data element when it improves comprehension. Keep body text left aligned, maintain consistent margins and gaps, and vary layouts only when the content benefits.

Use high-resolution, licensed imagery. Crop intentionally and avoid stretching. Maintain strong contrast, avoid color-only encoding, and include source notes for externally supplied facts or images. Reduce content or split slides instead of shrinking text until it is unreadable.
