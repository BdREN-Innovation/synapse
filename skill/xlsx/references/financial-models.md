# Financial models

Separate assumptions, calculations, and outputs. Label units and periods explicitly, keep scenario inputs in dedicated cells, and make projection formulas consistent across periods. Guard denominators and document circular calculations when they are intentional.

Unless the source workbook establishes another convention, distinguish hardcoded inputs, internal formulas, same-workbook links, and external links with consistent font colors. Use comments or nearby notes for assumptions and cite the source, period, and unit of imported figures.

Store percentages as decimal fractions, use parentheses for negatives, and show zero consistently. Use display formats rather than scaling the underlying values. Test sensitivities and reconciliation checks: balance sheets balance, cash flows bridge, totals tie to components, and scenario switches affect only intended outputs.
